HOW KMAP WORKS

From room codes to a walking route.

Choose a starting room and destination. KMap models campus corridors as connected points, then finds the route with the lowest estimated step count.

01

Choose two locations

Type a room code such as A012 or B106, or choose a room directly on the floor map. Use the floor buttons to view Ground, 1, 2, or 3.

02

Find the shortest route

KMap represents rooms, corridor connections, stairs, lifts, and entrances as a weighted graph. Dijkstra’s algorithm compares connected paths and selects the one with the lowest total cost.

03

Follow the directions

The result highlights route waypoints and gives approximate steps and turn prompts. Step counts and room positions are estimates from the supplied plans.
